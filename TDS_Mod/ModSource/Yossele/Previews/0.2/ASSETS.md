# Yossele 0.2: temporary art, what changed since 0.1 (2026-10-23)

> Since this was written: PC round 9 (9e, 160/0) built and ran all of it; `Debuff_Yossele_Daze` is Hallucinogen's own
> look since 2026-10-26 (pitfall #114); the Cordyfriend has its own temporary sheet since 2026-10-27
> (`../../Art/Temporary/README.md`). The renders below are as of 2026-10-23.

Everything here is still temporary art, generated by `Assets/YosseleMod/Editor` for the 2026-10-20 sheet
(`docs/scope/07_Vessel_Yossele.txt`). Only the files that changed are in this folder; every other render, the runtime
contract and the effect list are in `../0.1/ASSETS.md` and still hold. Checks: `check_editor.sh` passes with 0 errors
and `lite/run_preview.sh ... sma` runs clean. None of this has been built in Unity or seen in game yet.

## Sprite sheets

- `YosseleAttack-Sheet` (128x128, 4x4): the root-limb slam is gone. Yossele now fires its mouth laser: the hole glows
  dim, then bright, then flares lime as the shot leaves and the head kicks back a pixel (`sprite_YosseleAttack-Sheet.png`,
  one row per facing). The stock ranged AI plays it on each shot (the "Shoot" look).
- No other sheet changed. The move sheet's footstep now plays on frames 0 and 3, where each pair of root legs lands
  (`Code/03_Assets.cs`).

## Models

- `Yossele_Laser` (projectile, recentred, `models_Projectiles.png`): a short lime bolt along +z with a pale tip in
  front and a darker tail. The game turns a homing shot to face its goal.
- `Tower_Yosselebonsai` (`models_Dandelion.png`, last tile): the Tree at 60% in a wide, shallow terracotta dish with a
  hole and moss. Bonsai is its own building, so its model is not a `_` skin of the Tree.
- Cannon: `_Cordyceps5` (orange cordyceps clubs rising from the cap). `_Finish4` and `_Hallucinogen4` keep their 0.1
  looks under the level 4 keys.
- Tree: `_Dmds3` (a ring round the puff with eight seeds standing out on it), `_Bonsai4` (a cutting in a little pot at
  the foot), `_Rubbery4` (amber rubber seeds studding the puff), `_Storm5` (0.1's `_Storm4` look).
- Stonehenge: `_Easy3` (red thorns along the front vines), `_Roots4` (0.1's tangled roots, was `_Spaghetti4`),
  `_Obsidian4` (black glass stones), `_Monument5` (the hole glows green, a mossy capstone with flowers crowns the arc).
- Sapling: `_Forest3` (three little saplings round the pot), `_Mulch3` (a ring of bark chips and soil), `_Connected4`
  (four long surface roots to the tile corners), `_Harvest4` (fruit in the canopy), `_Fortifying5` (stone bands and
  leaning slabs), `_Softening5` (root tips crumbling ore nuggets). `_Selfish3` is gone with Selfish Roots.
- `Yossele_Vine_Ground.png`: rows 6 to 8 of the 20 x 20 texture fixed, so the horizontal stem leaves the right edge
  where it enters the left one and a row of vine tiles joins up. The preview renderer does not draw textures, so
  `models_Extras.png` shows the vine tile empty; check the join by eye in game.

## Effects

- `Debuff_Yossele_Marked` (new): Easy Target's mark, red and gold sparks circling over the foe's head. The stock
  `Debuff_Weakened` the code first used isn't loaded in a normal game (PC round 9).
- `Debuff_Yossele_Daze` is still generated but unused: Hallucinogen is a plain stun with the stock look.

## The Cordyfriend

No art of its own yet: it wears the Snoble's sprite, tinted fungus green on every client (`Code/13_Client.cs`
`Tint`), as the sheet allows until Dimitri's sprite arrives. It rises with a rustle and a spore puff and pops when it
falls. Its swing uses `Yossele_Thud`.

## Sounds

- `Yossele_Zap` (new, 0.03): a bright falling tone over a lower one, with a hiss. Plays on each laser shot.
- `Yossele_Thud`: now the Cordyfriend's swing.
- `Yossele_Mend`: now played (with `World_Yossele_Mend`) when Natural Remedy or Sustaining Practices heals, at most
  every 1.5 or 2 seconds per building.
- `Yossele_Landing` and `Yossele_Footstep` are gone: the intro and footsteps use the game's own sounds.
