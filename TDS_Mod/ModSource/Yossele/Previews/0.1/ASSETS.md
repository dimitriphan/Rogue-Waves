# Yossele 0.1: temporary art, asset list (2026-10-12)

This art is temporary and generated by `Assets/YosseleMod/Editor`. Every sprite frame is built from Dimitri's reshade
and back view, which are embedded pixel for pixel. Idle frame 0, the back frame and the icon match the source PNGs
exactly. Checks: `check_editor.sh` passes with 0 errors, and `lite/run_preview.sh ... sma` runs clean. These files are
the cloud renders. Nothing here has been built in Unity or seen in game yet. Shared copy:
`/mnt/project-files/concepts/yossele_2026-10-12/`.

## Sprite sheets (PPU 20, bottom-centre pivot; v1/v3 = v0/v2 mirrored)

- `Yossele-Sheet` (192x256): idle (rows 0-3) is a slow breath, sprout sway, blink and shoulder-leaf flutter. Move

- `YosseleIntroR-Sheet` (384x32, 12): it drops in with its eyes dark and lands heavily in dust. The eyes then glow,
  dim first, and the sprout perks up.
- `YosseleIdlejustidle-Sheet` (192x32): the idle v0 strip.
- `SYosseleSheet` (96x128, 16 px): the boat mini (10x13), with idle and move, front and back.
- `Yossele_Icon` (32x32): the reshade itself.
- `Yosselesplash` (128x128): leaf-green line art of the reshade at 5x, with the hole cut out and the eyes glowing.
- `Yosseleendstat1` (32x32, happy): root arms up, with flowers and leaves in the air.
- `Yosseleendstat2` (32x32, defeated): slumped and greyed, eyes dark, sprout drooping.
- `Readyup6yossele` (12x12): the face (hole, eyes, sprout, collar) in white brackets.
- `Yosseletabicon` (32x32): a white 1 px outline of the splash shapes, with the hole and two eye dots.

## Runtime contract (confirmed)

1. **Cannon:** every cannon model has `Tower_Yosselecannon*/Vis/Turret` with the same pivot, (0, 0.4) up from the
   ground, which is localPosition y -0.1. The Turret is the whole turning top (collar, barrel, stem and cap). Its pivot
   is the turret's base, which sits on top of the tub. It is not the underside of the cap. The barrel mouth points
   local -z. The swing stays within 0.41 of the axis.
2. **Stonehenge:** every henge model has `Tower_Yosselehenge*/Vis/Facing` at the tile's ground centre (localPosition y
   -0.5). It holds the whole model, with the front (holed stone and vines) on **+z**. There is no static `_Export`
   mesh. At rest the front faces away from the camera; the game turns the whole model by the placement orientation.
   `models_Henge_front.png` shows it turned toward the camera.
3. **Fruit:** `Yossele_Fruit` (all colours), `_Red`, `_Blue`, `_Yellow`.
4. **Seed and sporebomb:** `Yossele_Seed` is about 0.12 with its pivot at the centre. `Yossele_Sporebomb` is about 0.19
   with its pivot at the centre.
5. **Vine:** `Yossele_Vine` has its pivot at the tile centre on the ground and is a flat quad 0.01 above it.
6. **Sheets:** layouts as listed above. `World_Yossele_Dig` is in the effect list.

## Not made, or made differently

- **Debuff heights:** the contract asks for overhead markers 1.4-1.7 up. I followed the template's convention
  instead. The overhead meshes sit at 1.5-1.6 in model coordinates, as Mummer 1.0's verified marker does at 1.6, which
  puts them about 1.0-1.1 above the effect origin. If the game puts the debuff origin at the feet, raise them 0.5.
- **Spores stack count:** `Debuff_Yossele_Spores` doesn't show the count, as DESIGN.md says.
- **Stonehenge front on +z:** this follows the coordinator's runtime contract and overrides the brief's "front faces
  -z".
- **Mirroring:** v1 and v3 are mirrors of v0 and v2, as in the template. The shade band therefore sits on the left in
  the mirrored rows.
