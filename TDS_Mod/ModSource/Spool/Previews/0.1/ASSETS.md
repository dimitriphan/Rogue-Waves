# Spool 0.1: temporary art, asset list (2026-10-26)

This art is temporary and generated by `Assets/SpoolMod/Editor`. Every 32 px vessel frame is built from Dimitri's
reshade and its back view, which are embedded pixel for pixel and never recoloured: the rest frame and the icon equal
`concepts/vessels_2026-10-09/v3_flat/game_size/spool_reshade.png`, and the walk equals the concept
`concepts/walk_2026-10-12/game_size/SpoolWalk-Sheet.png` pixel for pixel (its generator is ported to C#). The boat
mini, the Crafted, the Thread Head, the towers, the effects and the sounds are drawn new. Checks: `check_editor.sh`
passes with 0 errors, and `lite/run_preview.sh ... sma` runs clean. These files are the cloud renders, made before any
build in Unity; the art has been in game since Spool's PC round 1 (`docs/TEMP_ART.md`).

**2026-11-05: sage, not purple.** At Dimitri's word every purple and lilac below is now his sage (plush `93CCA1` /
`60A871`, thread `CBE6D1` to `4F8C5E`; `ModSource/Spool/Art/Temporary/pastel_green_2026-11-05/README.md`). The renders
here were re-made then with `lite/run_preview.sh ... sm`; the five minion sheets are their files at 3x.

## Sprite sheets (PPU 20, bottom-centre pivot; v1/v3 = v0/v2 mirrored)

- `Spool-Sheet` (192x256): idle (rows 0-3) is a plush breath: the hood settles and lifts, a blink inside the mouth, the
  tongue lolls. Move (rows 4-7) is the hinged-hood walk: the hood slams shut on each footfall and flies open as the body
  drops; the severed arm drags on the floor.
- `SpoolAttack-Sheet` (128x128, 4x4): the right glove winds up beside the hood, swings out on a sleeve and comes down on
  the foe with a white swipe; the mouth gapes.
- `SpoolPatch-Sheet` (192x128, 6x4): PATCH. A needle with lilac thread in the right glove, two stitches out toward the
  mended unit, a sparkle.
- `SpoolFall-Sheet` (384x32, 12): falling with the hood flung open, gloves flapping, cotton and thread streaming up.
- `SpoolIntroR-Sheet` (384x32, 12): drops in with the hood open, lands in dust with it slammed shut, then peeks out,
  blinks and the tongue flops out.
- `SpoolIdlejustidle-Sheet` (192x32): the idle v0 strip.
- `SSpoolSheet` (96x128, 16 px): the boat mini (11x10), idle and move (the hood shuts on the footfalls), front and back.
- `Spoolstitched-Sheet`, `Spooldart-Sheet`, `Spoolseam-Sheet`, `Spoolmangled-Sheet` (160x64, 16 px; columns 0-5 move,
  frame 0 doubling as idle, 6-9 attack): Stitched is a short, stubby purple plush with Spool's zipper mouth and white
  gloves; Dart is thin and lilac with a needle held up, and throws it; Seam is round like a Snoble, white felt with a
  purple seam; Mangled is round and jagged, torn cotton with purple patches and odd eyes. From behind the faces are
  hidden. `Spoolmangled-Sheet` also dresses the Overstuffed Mangled.
- `Spoolhead-Sheet` (240x96, 24 px): the Thread Head, a big lilac ball of thread with Spool's mouth, two yellow eyes,
  white gloves, purple legs and a loose end trailing.
  Since 2026-10-27 these five sheets come from files, not the code: `SpoolBuilder.FileArt` takes
  `ModSource/Spool/Art/<name>.png` (final art) over `Art/Temporary/game_size/<name>.png` (temporary art, PR #44) over the
  code's sheet, when the file has the code sheet's size, and logs which it used. The temporary ones (redrawn 2026-10-28
  from Dimitri's own Spool sprite, at his ask) are lit and shaded with a real step (legs lift, gloves swing) and an
  attack with a wind-up, a smear and a hit spark; the Dart raises its needle behind its head and throws it. The Thread
  Head wears his zipper mouth pixel for pixel and punches with its leading glove; the four Crafted take his hood, mouth,
  tongue and gloves. The previews here show the files.
- `Spool_Icon` (32x32): the reshade itself.
- `Spoolsplash` (128x128): line art of the reshade at 5x in the eyes' yellow: the hood, the dark mouth with its teeth and
  eyes, the tongue, gloves, body, legs and the severed arm with its cotton.
- `Spoolendstat1` (32x32, happy): both gloves up, the hood open in a grin, thread and cotton in the air.
- `Spoolendstat2` (32x32, defeated): slumped, the hood shut over the eyes, gloves hanging, greyed.
- `Readyup6spool` (12x12): the face (hood, zipper mouth, eyes, tongue) in white brackets.
- `Spooltabicon` (32x32): a white 1 px outline of the splash shapes, with the mouth cut out and two eye dots.

## Models (low-poly, palette `Art/Models/Spool_Palette.png`; front faces -z)

- `Tower_Spoolstash`: a wicker basket heaped with yarn, two knitting needles stuck in, a loose end over the rim.
- `Tower_Spooltin`: a round blue cookie tin, gold bands and cookie dots, the lid propped behind it, full of spools, a
  little tomato pincushion and buttons. `_Recycle2` scraps and a ball beside it; `_Woolen2` a knitted lining over the
  rim; `_Discounted3` a price tag; `_Design3` a sewing pattern and needles stood up; `_Surprise4` a jack-in-the-box
  plush head on a spring, and a red bow on the lid.
- `Tower_Spoolcushion`: a big tomato pincushion in segments with a leaf cap and coloured pins. `_Sharper2` more and
  longer pins, needles out front; `_Unravel2` a loose thread trailing; `_Barrage3` pins all round; `_Stuffing3`
  stuffing out of a split seam; `_Overstuffed4` bigger, two puffs, felt patches; `_Burst4` three puffs and pins on the
  ground.
- `Tower_Spoolloom`: a frame loom with warp threads, a purple band half woven, the shuttle, a drop spindle on a peg and
  a pick at its foot. `_Direct2` a tall post with a reel and the thread running off home; `_Seam2` patches sewn on and
  a needle left in.
- `Spool_Pin`: the Cookie Tin's spot, a red-headed dressmaker's pin on a cross-stitch.
- `Spool_Needle`: the Dart's thrown needle, 0.42 long and about 0.05 thick so it reads in flight: a white point, a dark
  collar at the eye and a purple thread tail (2026-10-27, from `Art/Temporary/README.md`; was 0.26 x 0.02).
