Fathoms

Vessel rule

A Drilling Tower on an Ore Node goes one Fathom deeper for every Tide it stays there, down to Fathom 3. Each Fathom adds 50% aura and widens its Tremors by a tile.

Selling or moving a Drill brings it back to the surface. Deep nodes pay well, but only if you commit early: a Drill placed late in a run never reaches the bottom.

Sonar

Ping everything within 7 tiles. Enemies hit are Pinged for 6s and take 20% more damage. Drills in range quake at once, rooting every enemy in their Tremors for 1s. Cooldown 14s.

Basic attack, Wrench Swing: a slow, heavy melee swing that hits up to 2 enemies in front.

TOWERS & UNITS

House rules it follows

  • One art pixel is 6 screen pixels on the card. ARGO is 24 pixels wide and 28 tall, inside the 21 to 24 by 20 to 28 range of the shipped Vessels, in a 32×32 frame.
  • Crisp edges with no outline or soft rim. Each shape is a base colour with a stronger shade on its right and bottom edges and a near-base light on the left.
  • Eyes are 1×2 with the darker pixel on top. Pure white is kept for small accents: the glint on the porthole, the lamp, sparks.
  • Hands float at the sides; legs are 2 pixels wide. Units sit around 16 pixels, like Boutman's fish and SnoSno's Snoborne.
  • Towers are voxel models in the game, so they stay out of the sprite pack; the icons here stand in for them.

How ARGO is drawn

  • Smacky is the square, SnoSno the circle and MUMMER the triangle, so ARGO is the dome: an old brass diving helmet, a half-circle on straight sides.
  • The face is the porthole. Dark glass, two cyan eyes and one white glint. When ARGO pings, the eyes light up.
  • Brass on top, a sea-blue suit below, then lead boots. The orange rubber gloves are the only warm colour below the collar, so the hands read at a glance.
  • It's heavy. Each step lifts a boot two pixels and lands with a bob, and when it stands still, bubbles rise from the valve on its helmet.
  • Port and Starboard wear smaller domes with a red and a green light on top, the colours of a ship's left and right running lights. You pick between them often, so they have to be told apart at 16 pixels.

Stats follow the card's order: health, damage, attack interval, unit count, move speed, range and the $ stat. Values are placeholders sized against Smacky and Fie. ARGO trades move speed for health.

Nauts · Port and Starboard

Mob_Naut · unit

Small divers who live in the Mining Base. Select one, then a tower: it walks there and tunes it, for +20% attack speed, or +20% aura on an economy building. 20 health; a Naut that's knocked out swims home and is back in 8s.

One Naut per tower. They work on any player's towers, so a Mining Base helps the whole team. WICK's Lamppost also buffs teammates, but it's tethered in place; Nauts are units you keep moving.

Nauts and Fathoms · core loop

Send the crew, sink the drill

Pick a Naut, then a tower, to send it over. Tune your own Drill for more aura, or lend a Naut to a teammate's tower. Play a few Tides to watch the Drill go deeper. The lane here is walled off with Smacky's Walls, the way a team builds one on an open map.
