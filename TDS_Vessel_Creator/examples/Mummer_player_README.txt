Mummer, Strings Attached - a costume vessel mod for TDS - Tower Defense Strategy (v1.0.0)

INSTALL
1. In Steam, right-click TDS > Manage > Browse local files. This opens the TDS folder.
2. Extract this zip into that folder. You should end up with:
       TDS\Mods\MummerMod\MummerMod.info
       TDS\Mods\MummerMod\Windows\mummermod.assets  (plus 3 small files)
3. Start TDS. The mod is already switched on; you can check it in the Mods menu.
   Mummer appears on the boat in the vessel select. Works alongside Sprigg and Argo.

PLAYING ONLINE
Everyone in the lobby needs this same version installed and enabled, including the host.

THE VESSEL (Advanced)
A hand-sewn costume with a happy face drawn on the front, and something small in the
dark underneath. Melee with reach: a shadow arm swipes out from under the hem.

- HOOK (keys 6/7): pick an enemy within 5 tiles. It is reeled up to 2 tiles toward
  Mummer, held for a moment and Marked for 8 seconds. Puppets and the Cross go for
  Marked enemies first, and a Marked enemy pays 2 extra Thread when it falls.
  Recoil 9 seconds (the button greys out). Boss-weight enemies are Marked, not moved.
- THREAD: enemies felled near a Loom leave Scraps, spun into Thread (more for big
  ones). Half your Thread unravels at the start of each Tide (not the first), so spend it.

BUILDINGS
- Loom (40 yellow): collects Scraps of Thread from enemies felled nearby; picks at ore.
- Puppet Stage (50 red): stitches Understudies out of Thread, 2 at a time, leashed near
  the stage. The COSTUME button picks what it sews:
      Understudy (2 Thread) - plain felt brawler, always available.
      Ballista Understudy (4) - shoots felt bolts. Unlocked by scrapping a ranged enemy.
      Aegis Understudy (5) - a sturdy blocker that patches itself up. Unlocked by
      scrapping a heavy enemy.
- Marionette Cross (30 blue, 15 red): grabs an enemy on strings for 4 seconds. It
  can't move or fight back, and flails at its own neighbours instead.
- Curtain (20 blue): a trap enemies walk through and come out tangled: slowed, and too
  stage-struck to attack for a moment.

Every building has a branching upgrade tree (tiers 2 to 5) with a tier-5 finale:
Golden Thread, Standing Ovation, Puppet Master and Final Act.
The Journal has Mummer's own pages (vessel, buildings, every upgrade and the three
Understudies), and Mummer is in the vessel tabs.

UNINSTALL
Delete the TDS\Mods\MummerMod folder.
