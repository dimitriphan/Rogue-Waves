Rogue Waves VESSEL ▪ SPOTLIGHT

Control a Vessel, they are your anchor to the world. Five from the base game, two new ones in the works.

A fan concept for the Rogue Waves expansion mod. The five base vessels, their sprites and their numbers come from TDS - Tower Defense Strategy by Blockage; this page is not affiliated with Blockage. Base numbers and text are the game's own data (scope file 16, read from the game's code), and their sprites are the game's own sheets.

Spool and Yossele show only Dimitri's own work: his own sprites and his scope sheets 07, 08 and 11. Every default Claude picked in those sheets (a value written ~like this~) and every note Claude wrote is left out and shown as work in progress, and none of Claude's concept or test-build art is used.
