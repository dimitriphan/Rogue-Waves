### 2.3 What it means for the fix

- Across both runs: map cards with a picture froze 3 of 50 openings, the mode's plain dialog 1 of 20, stock 0 of 20.
  The check (at most one more than stock in 10) fails or passes by chance at that rate, so it can't prove a fix either
  way.
- The time is the render thread's (or the GPU's, which the render thread waits on), not script, layout or text. What
  the mod's dialog gives it to draw that the stock one doesn't is the lead: the large card picture (its one missed
  refresh per opening points there too), a new material or mask per opening. Pitfall #138's 62-70 ms frames on 2 of 10
  openings (the title's autosize turned off) have the same shape and may be the same stall.
- Next probes, cheapest first: the split again with the card picture's `Image` given the default UI material, then
  with the picture's texture made non-readable or at a power-of-two size; `QualitySettings.vSyncCount` 0 to see whether
  the stall is the present (whole refreshes) or the render itself.
