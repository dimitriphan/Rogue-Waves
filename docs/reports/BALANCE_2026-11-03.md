# Balance study 2026-11-03: Yossele and Spool, simulated

> Frozen as written. Asked by Dimitri ("Run 3, 4, and 5", job 3 of a list of long overnight jobs: a
> long virtual play test of Yossele and Spool with a balance report and tuning proposals). Cloud only: no PC round, no
> Remote Control. Nothing in the mods changed; every number below is a **proposal** until Dimitri picks it.

**In one paragraph.** A simulator of a map Tide by Tide (`balance_2026-11-03/`) was calibrated against the 2026-10-26
play test and run about 13,000 times: three stock maps, two kinds of player, Smacky as the stock yardstick, about 35
tuning options. **Yossele needs no change:** it is at least as strong as Smacky on every map, Red is the scarce colour
for every vessel (kills pay only Red), and the Blue that piled up in the play test came from Purlest's Opal and the
three Coin Cardinals the test had equipped; moving Yossele's costs from Red to Blue makes it lose Woozy. **Spool needs
two things.** Its thin first Tide is a missing wall: a player who shuts the gap with three Pin Cushions takes no core
damage in Tides 1-4 on Purlest and Woozy, and no start-kit number (more Thread, free Stitched, a cheaper Tin) saves a
player who doesn't. Its real gap is later: the Maul at Tide 10 kills every Crafted near the line in one slam and a Pin
Cushion in two, and the Tempered Aegis at Tide 11 outlast its damage, so it loses Purlest at Tide 11 every time, where
Smacky and Yossele win. Three numbers close the gap (§8): **Dart Range 3.5 → 4.5, 6 Crafted a Tin instead of 4, Stitched
Damage 2 → 3 and Dart
2.5 → 3.5**. With them Spool wins Purlest and lasts to Tide 11 on Woozy, a Tide short of Smacky.

## The proposals at a glance

| # | Vessel | Change | From → to | Why | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Spool | Dart Range | 3.5 → 4.5 | Darts 2 tiles behind the Cushion line then reach a Maul at the line from outside its 2.5-tile slam | medium: needs M7 (§9) |
| P2 | Spool | Cookie Tin "Crafted alive at once" | 4 → 6 | more hands for the same Tin; carries Woozy's mid game | medium |
| P3 | Spool | Stitched Damage, Dart Damage | 2 → 3, 2.5 → 3.5 | the Crafted's damage never grows; Tide 11-14's health does | medium |
| P4 | Spool | the journal and tooltip say "wall the path with Pin Cushions first" | text | the early game is the wall, not the numbers | high |
| - | Yossele | nothing | | at or above Smacky on every map; keep the 2026-10-28 defaults (PLAN Decisions: laser mines ore, costs as written) | high |

P1-P3 are one package: alone, each moves Spool's Purlest loss from Tide 11 to Tide 12-13 at best; together they win it
(§8). All three are `~defaults~` Claude picked in scope `08`, not numbers Dimitri wrote, but they change how Spool
plays, so they wait for his word. The knobs that did little are in §5.

## 1. How it was done

The simulator (`balance_2026-11-03/sim.py`, Python, no game code in it) plays one map Tide by Tide at 0.1 s steps on
the mode's Tide table. Today the mode copies `Mode_Normal` ("Downpour": 180 s Tides, 30 s breaks, 45 s before Tide 1)
and the scope wins a map after Tide 15, so every run stops there. Each foe walks its lane from its enemy core to a
player core. The numbers are the game's, from the RogueLab dumps in `TDS_Vessel_Creator/reference/` and the decompiled
code (read in the scratchpad, never committed):

| Piece | How the simulator has it |
|---|---|
| Foes | Dagger, Spear, Aegis (heals 2/s after 5 s unhit), Ballista, Maul (its slam hits up to 50 things within 2.5 tiles every 6.7 s), Onager, Claymore; Hardened (Health ×4), Tempered (Health and Damage ×2, turns on a unit or building within 5 tiles), Superheated (×3, Speed ×2.25). Map difficulty scales nothing |
| Fighting | A walker fights only when blocked or at the core (`Mob_Combat` looks for a target only while idle); the Maul slams on the move; the Ballista and the Onager shoot what is in range while walking. Each enemy core hands out reachable player cores round robin, spawn by spawn |
| Walls | Foes stop at a wall line only while the whole gap is shut (3 buildings by default; 1-4 tried in §6). The game has no "path still open" check (kit `docs/16` §2): a foe walks round a wall when it can |
| Maps | **Purlest** (`3_Opal`): one core, lanes of 70, 51 and 39 tiles; the Opal 11 tiles out pays about 9.5 aura a gatherer strike, Red, Blue, all, Yellow by Tide. **Woozy**: one core, lanes of 93 and 90; three nodes 5 tiles out pay about 1 a strike. **The Hunt**: two cores (the Subcore takes every other spawn), lanes of 44-61, one Red and Yellow node 4 tiles out. Lane lengths and payouts are read off the 2026-10-26 logs |
| Income | Red from kills (Dagger and Spear 1, Aegis and Ballista 3, Maul and Onager 10); ore; the Coin Cardinals when a run says so |
| Yossele | 0.2.0-dev as built (`DESIGN.md`): laser, Mushroom Cannon (spores, Growth, every upgrade), Dandelion Tree (seeds, Packets, Picky, Demand, D-MDS, Rubbery, Storm), Stonehenge (vines, Blockade, Remedy, Carnivore, Monument, Obsidian), Pachira Sapling (mining, Focused, Grafted, fruit) |
| Spool | 0.1.0-dev as built (`TDS_Mod/ModSource/Spool/DESIGN.md`): Thread drops (1, 3 large, +2 elite, at most 5, 15 s on the ground), Yarn Stash (cap 50, rebuilt free), Cookie Tin, Stitched, Darts, Thread Head (collects 3 tiles, carries 20, a 6 s trip home), Pin Cushion (reflects 20%, 1 Thread per 25 damage), Loom (1 Thread every 6 s), PATCH, Mangled, every upgrade |
| Smacky | the stock yardstick: archers, bombers, walls, the Mining Hut, their upgrades |

**Two players per vessel.** The **bot** plays like the 2026-10-26 virtual player: builds beside the last stretch, buys
on fixed routes, never shuts the path. It is the one calibrated against the play test and the one the PC harness
plays. The **smart** player plays like a fair human: shuts the gap in front of the core (three Stonehenges, Pin
Cushions or Walls) and rebuilds it, saves for the Tree, buys what pays, keeps Cookie Tins more than 5 tiles behind the
line (their TARGET reaches 8), builds Tins while Red allows. Every result is the smart player with no Cardinals
unless it says otherwise. Each setup ran 60 or 100 times with different seeds (foe speed ±10%, spawn spread, every
chance roll); percentages are shares of those runs.

**One fix during the study.** The first batch let a Tempered foe hit its target from wherever it turned on it; Spool's
short-range Crafted then could not reach it. The simulator now walks it up to its target, and stops it at a shut line.
Every result below is from the fixed model (Yossele's Woozy result went from 3% wins to 97%; Smacky's from losing at
Tide 11 to Tide 12).

## 2. Calibration against the 2026-10-26 play test

The play test's virtual player had **three Coin Cardinals equipped** (Green, Blue and Yellow Coin, the lobby loadout
`roguelab_round1.txt` line 153 also shows): start R56 B81 Y81 instead of 50 each, then at Tide n +n Red, +7n Blue and
+7n Yellow. By Tide 15 that is 120 Red and 840 each of Blue and Yellow. Calibration runs use them; every other run
doesn't unless it says Coins, since the mode's start has no Cardinals.

| Run (bot, Coins on, 100 runs) | Simulated | Play test |
|---|---|---|
| Yossele, Purlest, to Tide 8 | clean in 100% (core never hit) | clean |
| Yossele, The Hunt, to Tide 10 (no ore: the play test's Sapling struck nothing, §9 M4) | lost at Tide 6 in 94% (5: 4%, 7: 2%) | lost at Tide 7 |
| Spool, Purlest, to Tide 6 | lost at Tide 4 in 96% (3: 4%) | lost at Tides 4, 1 and 2 |

The model is about a Tide harsh on The Hunt and a little kind to Spool's first Tides. Read every result as a
comparison: each option against the vessel's own baseline, each vessel against Smacky in the same model.

### The first Tides: the wall, not the start kit

The smart player's three Pin Cushions take **no core damage in Tides 1-4 on Purlest and Woozy in any of 60 runs**. The
bot never shuts the path, and foes walk past Crafted that stand on it: a walker doesn't stop to fight, and a Crafted
chasing a faster foe rarely lands a hit (the 2026-10-26 logs: Crafted damage came from foes standing at the core).
Start-kit options against the bot (60 runs each):

| Option | Bot, Purlest | Bot, Woozy | Bot, The Hunt |
|---|---|---|---|
| As written | lost T4 (87%) | lost T2 (75%) | lost T1 |
| Start Thread 10 → 20 or 30 | lost T4 | lost T3 | lost T1 |
| 1 or 2 free Stitched with the first Tin | lost T4 | lost T3 | lost T1 |
| First Tin Red 50 → 30 or 40 | lost T4 | lost T2 | lost T1 |
| Stitched cost 10 → 8 Thread | lost T4 | lost T2-3 | lost T1 |
| Pin Cushion Blue 20 → 15 | lost T4 | lost T2 | lost T1 |
| Stitched block foes like a wall (a design change) | alive at T4 100%, lost T5 | lost T2 | lost T1 |
| Crafted land 4 in 10 hits on walkers instead of 1 (needs the game checked, §9 M6) | alive at T4 100%, lost T5 | lost T2 | lost T1 |

**Read.** No number fixes a Spool who doesn't wall. Proposal P4 is words: the journal and the Pin Cushion's tooltip
say to shut the path with Cushions first (scope `08` already calls the Cushion "Spool's main line of defense"), and the
PC harness's Spool plays that way (§9).

One start-kit number does help the smart player on Woozy: **Stitched at 8 Thread** (Discounted Material's price)
moves the loss from Tide 8 to Tide 10 (alive at Tide 8: 20% → 90%). It works the same way P2 does (more Crafted
for the Thread a poor map drops).

### Tides 10 and 11: the gap

Purlest is lost at Tide 11 in every run, the same way each time. At Tide 10 the Maul walks into the line: its slam
(Damage 50, every 6.7 s, everything within 2.5 tiles) kills every Stitched (20, 30 with Woolen Lining), Dart (12),
Mangled and Thread Head near the line and a Pin Cushion (100) in two. Nothing Spool has stands outside the slam and
reaches it, so the Maul lives into Tide 11 while Spool rebuilds Cushions until Blue runs out. Then fifteen Tempered
Aegis (160 health, 30 damage) break the line and kill the core in seconds. Spool's damage never grows: a Cookie Tin's
upgrades add no Damage to its Crafted, while foes' health goes from 40 (Hardened Dagger) to 320 (Hardened Aegis).

Late-game options (smart, 60 runs; "alive T10" is the share still alive at the end of Tide 10):

| Option | Purlest | Woozy |
|---|---|---|
| As written | lost T11 (100%) | lost T8 (47%), T7; alive T10 0% |
| Dart Range 3.5 → 4.5, every Tin on Darts | lost T12 (73%), T13 | same (Spool doesn't live to buy New Design) |
| Stitched 3, Dart 3.5 Damage | lost T11 (95%) | lost T11 (65%), T10; alive T10 65% |
| Stitched 4, Dart 4.5 Damage | lost T12 (57%), T11 | lost T11 (100%) |
| Crafted +0.5 Damage per Tin upgrade | lost T11 (73%), T12 | lost T10 (58%) |
| Crafted +1 Damage per Tin upgrade | wins 32% | lost T11 (48%), T10 |
| Dart Range 4.5 and +1 Damage per Tin upgrade | wins 72% | lost T10-11 |
| 6 Crafted a Tin | lost T11 | lost T11 (87%); alive T10 87% |
| Pin Cushion Health 100 → 150 | lost T11 (87%) | lost T8 |
| Thread drop +1 | lost T11 | lost T10 (77%) |
| Looms spin twice as fast | lost T11 | lost T10 (92%) |
| **P1 + P2 + P3** (Range 4.5, 6 a Tin, Damage +1) | see §8 | see §8 |

**Read.** Three things are short, and each knob fixes only its own: reach past the Maul's slam (Dart Range), hands on
a poor map (Crafted a Tin), and damage that keeps up with Tide 11-14 health (Crafted Damage). On Purlest Spool runs out
of damage, not Thread: more Thread, faster Looms or sturdier Cushions change nothing there. On Woozy it is short of
both, so more Thread (a drop +1, Looms twice as fast, Stitched at 8) gets it to Tide 10 and more Crafted or damage to
Tide 11. All three numbers are
`~defaults~` Claude picked in scope `08`, not numbers Dimitri wrote, and his own line for the Cookie Tin is "Main source
of damage".

### The Hunt

Spool loses The Hunt at Tide 2 in every option tried. Two cores need two lines and two Tins from a 50/50/50 start, and
The Hunt's one node pays no Blue, so the second line is never built (cheaper Cushions, a free first Tin, more Thread:
still Tide 1-4). Smacky and Yossele lose it at Tides 5 and 6 in this model too, so it is the hardest map for everyone;
Spool is simply the one with no answer before Tide 2. A real fix is a design question, not a number: Spool's sheet
could give the Yarn Stash a starting Pin Cushion or two, or the mode's map pick could keep two-core maps off a
campaign's first maps. Neither is proposed here.

### Thread and Yellow

On Purlest Spool's Thread is roughly balanced (779 dropped, 468 spun, 880 spent; 224 expire on the ground). Yellow is
not: 3176 is left at Tide 15, since only the Loom (40, upgrades 30) takes it. The sheet's open lines (`! NEEDS YARN
STASH UPGRADES !`, the Loom's levels 2-3) are the natural Yellow sink; no number is proposed.

both, 77% on Woozy at width 4). Smacky moves the most (Woozy lost at Tide 10 or 12). The conclusions don't hinge on it.

|---|---|---|---|

## 7. What the model cannot tell

- **Space.** A lane is a line; buildings stand in a cluster at the kill zone. Real maps have side paths, heights and
  corners where a foe can be held or slip past. Where Tins, Darts and cannons stand matters more in game than here.
- **The vessel's own feet.** Yossele and Spool stand at a spot; a player walks them to Thread, fruit and trouble.
- **Crafted against walkers.** The model lands 1 hit in 10 on a walking foe (fitted to the 2026-10-26 logs, where
  Crafted damage came from foes standing at the core). If the game is kinder, the bot's early game is better than §5
  says (M6 checks it).
- **The Hunt's Orange node and the Opal's colours** are read off one play test each (M2-M4).
- **Directions, Cardinals beyond the Coins, Pure Aura, the sea weather, online play** are all left out. Each adds
  power or income on top; none of them is in the first release.
- **Absolute numbers.** The model is about a Tide harsh on The Hunt (§2). Trust the comparisons, not the Tide count.

Alone, Dart Range moves Purlest's loss to Tide 12-13, while 6 a Tin and +1 Damage each leave it at Tide 11. Dart Range
with either of the others reaches Tide 13-14 (an eight-seed look); 6 a Tin with +1 Damage but no Range still loses at

The package leaves Spool's early game and The Hunt where they were: those are P4 (the wall, in words) and the design
question in §5. It also leaves Spool a Tide behind Smacky on Woozy, which fits an "Advanced" vessel on an ore-poor map.

**Spool, P4** (no number): one sentence in the journal page and the Pin Cushion's tooltip, for example "Wall the path
with Pin Cushions first: Crafted fight best behind them." Dimitri's wording wins.

**Yossele: no change.** Keep the 2026-10-28 defaults (PLAN Decisions: the laser keeps mining ore; scope `07`'s
costs, healths and lines as written). Two notes for his own pass: Mushroom Cannon at Red 12 is the lever if the early
game should feel looser (The Hunt Tide 6 → 8-9, nothing lost elsewhere); a line in D-MDS's journal entry that it stops
the Tree's damage on maps without shooters (the 2026-10-26 report's trap).

**Not proposed, tried and dropped:** more start Thread, free Stitched, a cheaper first Tin or Pin Cushion, more
Thread per kill, faster Looms, sturdier Pin Cushions (Spool); moving Yossele's costs from Red to Blue, a cheaper Tree,
the Tree a Tide later.
