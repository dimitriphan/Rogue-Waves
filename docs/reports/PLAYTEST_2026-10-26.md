# Play test 2026-10-26: Yossele 0.2.0-dev and Spool 0.1.0-dev, played through the HUD

Dimitri asked (2026-10-26): "Do a test run of Yossele and Spool as if you were a player testing these characters." The
self-tests send every command themselves, so the HUD (build bar, upgrade entries, pickers, SELL, toggles) had never been
clicked. This report covers what a player-style run of both vessels showed on three stock maps, what it found, the two
fixes it made, and what still needs Dimitri's own eye. Everything here ran in game on the PC; nothing is read only.

**Results in one paragraph.** Two real bugs, both fixed on their PR branches and checked in game: the Thread Head's panel
never drew (an unknown key name threw on every click), and Hallucinogen's stun look is a stock effect only some maps load.
31 of Yossele's 32 upgrades and all 15 of Spool's were bought by clicking their entries; the build bars, TARGET, SPOT,
RETURN, MEND, PATCH, SELL and fast-forward all work by hand. Balance: Yossele held Purlest to Tide 8 on its own income
without the core taking a hit and lasted to Tide 7 on The Hunt; Spool lost Purlest at Tides 4, 1 and 2 in three tries,
and its first Tide is the problem.

## 1. How it was played

- **The hand.** No desktop control was available and kit `docs/08` forbids desktop screenshots, so the play builds carry a
  virtual player (`ModSource/Shared/ModKitPlay.cs`, kit 1.6 add-on, compiled only with `MODKIT_PLAY`): it queues Input
  System state events on the game's own `Mouse.current` and `Keyboard.current`. The game reads its world pointer from
  `Mouse.current` (`Game_Client_Mouseobject.Update`), its keys and clicks from Input System actions (`Bla_Input`), and the
  HUD through `InputSystemUIInputModule`, so these events take the same path a player's hand does. Every build, upgrade,
  sell and command below was a click on the HUD or the map, or a key (F, R, 1-4, Esc). Only when a click didn't take was
  the button's own command sent instead, logged `FALLBACK` and counted (section 4).
- **Aiming.** A tile's or a building's screen point is often a neighbour's side face on tall maps, so each click walks the
  pointer in a small spiral until the game's own pointer reports the wanted tile or thing, the way a player looks before
  clicking. The camera was moved by setting `Game_Cameracontroller.Movepos` (a stand-in for the pan keys; the camera
  controls themselves were not tested).
- **Maps.** The PC's world map offers six maps, all mode "Downpour", 30 Tides: Purlest (`3_Opal`, difficulty 4), Woozy
  (`3B_Woozy`, 5), The Hunt (`3B_Thehunt`, 7), Kong, Manafold, Teri Tori. "Own income" runs play on the map's own aura.
  "Sandbox" runs add 2500 (Yossele) or 1500 (Spool) of each aura at every Tide, the Sandbox Cardinal's money button, and
  spawn foes of a kind at the spawner (its other button) so every upgrade gets bought and meets shooters and heavies: Tide 3
  Ballistas, Tide 4 Onagers, then Mauls, Excaliburs, Claymores, Aegis.
- **Speed.** The bot clicks the HUD's fast-forward (Time.timeScale 1.5). A Tide lasts 180 s of game time.
- **Strategy.** Simple and stated in the play scripts (`Code/98_Play.cs` of each vessel): the foes' paths are read from the
  game (`Helper_Navtools`) and buildings go beside the last stretch before the core; gatherers go beside the ore nearest the
  core; upgrades follow fixed routes so every branch is bought on a sandbox map. **The balance numbers are what this bot
  got, not what a skilled player gets**; they are useful as a comparison between the two vessels played the same way.

### Bug found and fixed: Hallucinogen's stun look

`YosseleSpores.Stun` (`08_Cannon.cs`) used the stock `Debuff_Stun`. It is loaded on The Hunt (so the self-test's
`DebuffReport` passed there) but not on Woozy: there each of the 10 stuns logged "Could not retrieve Vfx/Debuff_Stun The
GROUP exists, but item does not" and showed nothing. Fixed on PR #41: Hallucinogen now uses Yossele's own
`Debuff_Yossele_Daze` (the purple swirl and stars that was generated for it and left unused), `YosseleData.VfxDaze`; the
self-test's check follows. Pitfall #114 records that stock looks load per map. Checked in game: the last Woozy run had 9
stuns and no "Could not retrieve" line (the look itself wasn't caught in a capture). `Debuff_Slow` (the vines' slow) is
the same kind of risk but is in Smacky's group, which Yossele always loads, and no run logged it missing (44 slows).

### The laser mines

The vessel's damage with no foe felled (Woozy: 433 by Tide 6, 802 by Tide 7, 0 kills; The Hunt: 139 with 7 kills) says
the stock ranged AI shoots the ore beside the core, as round 9e's notes guessed. Ore pays every player, so it is a quiet
income, not a bug; whether Yossele should do it is Dimitri's call.

- The core and the vessel never took damage. Cannons carry: at Tide 8 the two oldest had 752 and 664 damage; the Tree 105,
  the vessel 57 (it stands at the core, behind the cannons).
- **Blue piles up** (803 at Tide 8) because only the Stonehenge and a few late upgrades spend it, while every cannon and its
  upgrades want Red. A player short of Red early has little to spend Blue on.
- **D-MDS on a map without shooters stops the Tree** (its damage froze at Tide 5 on Purlest: Daggers, Spears, Aegis shoot
  nothing). Its line says so, but it is an easy trap at level 3; worth a word in the journal or the tooltip.
- **The vines meet foes only where foes get close**: none slowed on Woozy (the cannons' range of 9-11 kills Daggers first),
  36 on The Hunt, where foes reach the Stonehenges.

**The Hunt (difficulty 7), own income: lost at Tide 7.** The Subcore (the core foes reach first there) went 100 -> 55 at
Tide 6 -> 25 at Tide 7 and fell; the vessel fell once at Tide 6; 144 foes felled (Dagger 93, Spear 31, Aegis 7). Red was
the limit all map long (10-46 at each Tide's start): the bot spent every 15 Red on another cannon and never banked the 50
for a Tree. A player who saves for the Tree, and walls the Subcore's approach, likely does better; the Red squeeze is the
same one Purlest showed.

**Woozy, sandbox:** with every route bought, the base held to Tide 7 against the spawned Ballistas, Onagers, Mauls,
Excaliburs and Claymores (core untouched; the newest Farout cannon did 2404 damage and 87 kills). In an earlier sandbox
run that sold the D-MDS Tree at Tide 5 to replant it, 2 Excaliburs and 2 Claymores flattened the base a Tide later: D-MDS
is what holds against heavy shooters.

### Bug found and fixed: the Thread Head's panel

Every click on a Thread Head threw `KeyNotFoundException: The given key 'Quickaction2' was not present in the dictionary`
in `Game_Selection.Addcommand` (13 times in one run), so its panel never drew: no SPOT, RETURN, MEND or upgrade entries,
and the HUD kept the last selection's buttons. `Addcommand` looks each of a command's keybinds up in `Bl_Inputs.Actions`,
which holds `Quickaction` and `1`-`8` only, and `BuildCommands` gave RETURN `{"Quickaction2", "4"}` and MEND
`{"Quickaction3", "3"}`. Fixed on PR #42: RETURN `{"4"}`, MEND `{"3"}`. After the fix: no exception in two runs, and SPOT,
RETURN, MEND and the Head's upgrades were used by hand. The Spool thread adds the pitfall (#113) and a self-test check.

- **The first Tide is the problem.** After the free Stash and the first Tin (Red 50 of the 56 a map starts with), the Stash's
  10 Thread buys one Stitched (20 health, 2 damage). Thread comes 1 per Dagger felled, so the second Stitched needs ten

  Red 30 and takes no hit. Dimitri's call; ideas: more starting Thread (20-30), a free first Stitched, or the first Tin
  cheaper.
- **Pin Cushions on the path were walked round** on Purlest (0 damage taken in every run there): foes path round a wall
  where they can, so a Cushion is only hit where it seals a lane. Worth saying in its line, or making foes target it.
- The Thread Head's panel also has the stock **MOVE** next to SPOT: two ways to move it; whether MOVE should go is a
  design question.

Runs after the aiming went in (P6 on); a fallback is the button's command sent by the host after a click didn't take.

| Run | Builds by hand (fallback) | Upgrades (fallback) | Sells (failed) | Commands (not taken) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P6 Yossele, Woozy sandbox | 12 (2) | 44 (0) | 1 (0) | - |
| P10 Yossele, The Hunt + Woozy sandbox | 29 (4) | 53 (0) | 1 (0) | - |
| P9 Spool, Purlest + Woozy sandbox | 15 (3) | 22 (2) | 0 | 22 (0) |
| P11 Spool, Woozy sandbox | 10 (1) | 18 (1) | 2 (2) | 8 (1) |

Every fallback traced to the bot: an occluded tile the aim didn't reach (it then clicked where it was, so a few buildings
went up a tile or two off the spot it meant), a moving unit it couldn't select, or a Thread Head that came back from a
death and was asked for its other level-2 upgrade (the host's command skips the HUD's offer check, which a client's
doesn't: not a bug, but that Head ended with both).

- **Art:** both vessels' temporary art against his sprites; the Cordyfriend, the fruit, Hallucinogen's daze, Thread tufts

- **Not reachable here:** Growth's 5-a-Tide rule (no short map on this world map), a Bulwark against the Monument (no
  Bulwark on these maps in 7 Tides, and Woozy doesn't load one), fruit on other node kinds, anything online (Connected
  Roots, clients' looks).
