| New Mechanics |
	# The systems the expansion adds to every map of the Rogue Waves mode: [Directions], [Pure Aura], [Fund Transfer] and the [Shopkeeper].
	# Cardinal rules live in 12_Cardinals.txt, and the mode's own rules in 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt.

	[Directions]
		# A new gameplay altering mechanic specific to each player. Directions can provide normal bonuses to characters, or create fundamental changes to how a vessel functions.
		- Offered: 3 are offered and 1 is picked before each map
		- Ranks: a Direction a player already holds can be offered again as its next rank (II, III) / So about 7 for any vessel and 4 per new vessel last a 10-map campaign \
		- Rank costs: rank II costs Pure Aura 20, and its final rank, III, costs Pure Aura 30 / Bought at {VENDCINT} \
		- Shop: the shop sells only the next rank of a Direction the vessel already holds
		- Kept: each player's Directions sit on their own vessel and come back at the start of every map. They are saved with the campaign
		- Base game vessels: get their own Directions too, besides the Any ones
		- Names: a plain name that matches its Look line; a heading or a course is optional
		- List: -> 11_Directions.txt, one Direction template per Direction, under its vessel

	[Pure Aura]
		# A new currency earned in game to purchase [Directions] and [Cardinals]. Can be earned by surviving tides and by accepting and completing optional objectives. Each player has their own amount of [Pure Aura].
		- Per tide: 3
		- Per bonus tide: 2
		- Weather map: pays extra each tide -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Sea Weather]
		- Objectives: each pays its own amount -> 14_Optional_Objectives.txt
		- Shown as: a pearl white counter on each player's vessel, a colour none of the base auras use
		- Spent on: [Cardinals] and ranks of [Directions], at {VENDCINT}

	[Fund Transfer]
		# Players are able to send each other a limited amount of aura inbetween waves at {VENDCINT}, allowing for more cooperative play and specialization into roles.
		- This is the expansion's "Streamlined Economy Changes"
		- What can be sent: only base aura, never [Pure Aura]
		- Limit: each player can send up to 50 times the coming tide's number per break, in any mix of colours / 50 before Tide 1, and 250 in the break before Tide 5 (125 red and 125 blue, or 250 blue) \
		- Where: at {VENDCINT}

	[Shopkeeper]
		{VENDCINT} / A sentient vending machine \
			- Arrives: a unit that falls from the sky near the vessels' spawn point at the start of every break, the opening one before Tide 1 included, the way the vessels land
			- Leaves: at the end of the break it jumps into the sky and disappears / A break lasts 60 seconds -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Tides and Leaving] \
			- Sells: [Cardinals], and the next rank of [Directions] the vessel already holds, for [Pure Aura] / A Cardinal's cost is on its own sheet in 12_Cardinals.txt \
			- Also: [Fund Transfer] is done at it too
			- Objectives: a new optional objective is offered at it in every break -> 14_Optional_Objectives.txt
