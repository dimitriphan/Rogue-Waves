	[Summary]
		The Rogue Waves is an expansive mod revolving around roguelike mechanics and new content. New vessels and Cardinals are included, along with a new roguelike mode. In this mode, players join together and play a campaign of 10 maps in a row with increasing difficulty and new modifiers / enemies that change with each campaign. Progress is saved between maps, so a campaign can be played over several sessions. Players can empower themselves using new mechanics and upgrades, purchasable with a new currency earned each tide and from optional objectives given to the player: [Directions] are picked before each map, and their ranks are bought at {VENDCINT}. Completing a campaign unlocks a harder difficulty modifier for future campaigns. Score does not count in this mode.

	[Campaign]
		- Maps: 10 in a row, with increasing difficulty
		- Stretches: 2 of 5 maps / The 5th and 10th rows of the sea chart close a stretch \
		- Vessels: players must select their vessel first before the Rogue Waves begins, as they are locked into their choice
		- Start: the host starts a campaign from the Rogue Waves button on the main menu (New or Continue), which takes them to the boat with the mode already set, and invites friends there
		- Saving: progress is saved between maps, so a campaign can be played over several sessions
		- Continue: when the host picks the campaign up again with Continue on the main menu's Rogue Waves button, each player gets back their vessel, [Pure Aura], [Directions] and [Trinkets], and the team its [Cardinals]
		- Won: if the players clear Tide 15 on the final map and leave it, they complete the campaign and a new Depth level is unlocked / Map 10 ends like every map, so the campaign is won by leaving map 10 after Tide 15 or later \ /~ Depth is on hold: until it is built, nothing unlocks \
		- Lost: a failed campaign keeps nothing
		- Score: does not count in this mode
		- Changes per campaign: new modifiers / enemies that change with each campaign: each campaign's seed picks 2 of the 4 new modifiers and 2 of the stock elites (Armor, Health, Red, Explode, Aegis), none is on for map 1, one comes on from map 2 and one more every second map (all four by map 8), on one foe in five of each row, taken in turn from Tide to Tide; a stock elite a map can't load is replaced by the next one outside the four, so no map plays easier than its number /~ Your "default for all" (2026-11-05, docs/reports/OPEN_DECISIONS_2026-11-05b.md item 4); from map 2 and in turn: your "all proposals" (F4), docs/reports/BALANCE_SWEEP_2026-11-05.md section 2 \

	[Difficulty]
		- Each map: harder than the one before: more enemy modifiers, higher spawn rates, harder optional objectives and more enemy health
		- Numbers: map k's Tide 15 plays the base game's Tide ~15, 18, 20, 21, 21, 24, 24, 24, 24, 24~ (maps 1 to 10), its Tides 1-14 the rows spread before it; foes +6% a map, +50% per player past the first; spawn intervals -3% a map; foe health x~2.6~ on map 1 and 0.08 more a map (x~3.32~ on map 10), then ~+2%~ for every Cardinal, Trinket copy and Direction rank the party holds when the map starts (the lobby's picks not counted), all multiplying, and on top of an elite's own; bonus Tides play the rows after Tide 15's with the map's scaling, and from row 29 on each bonus Tide +10% foes on the last /~ Your "Starting set S2, all proposals" (2026-11-05), tuned by the balance sweep to the review's target: about 0.8 of a base game run on map 1, 1.0 by map 3, 1.45 on map 10 (mid case). docs/reports/BALANCE_SWEEP_2026-11-05.md section 2 \

	[Retries]
		- Per campaign: 3
		- Losing a map: uses one of a limited number of retries: the team replays that map from the last save. With no retries left, a lost map ends the campaign
		- The host quitting: the host quitting during a map counts as losing it. Only the host quitting during a map spends a retry
		- A friend quitting: a friend who drops or quits doesn't spend one: they can rejoin the running map (-> 03_Bug_Fix.txt), and a friend who drops while the map loads comes back on the boat
		- Dying in a bonus tide: doesn't lose the map: a map counts as won once Tide 15 is cleared, no retry is spent, and only the tide in progress pays nothing
		- After a loss: the boat sails back to the lost map by itself, and only that map can be picked until it is won
		- Status: built, and a lost map, its retry and Continue passed on the PC /~ docs/reports/BUILD_2026-11-03.md \

	[Depth] / Ascension levels \
		- Levels: 5
		- Each level: adds a change along with the previous depth levels
		- Named: Depth 1, Depth 2 and so on
		- Between sessions: meant to carry over
		- Unlocked by: completing a campaign -> [Campaign] Won
		- Changes: / Blank until Depth is designed \
		- Status: on hold until the core gameplay loop is settled

	[Tides and Leaving]
		- Breaks: 60 seconds between tides
		- Map won: once Tide 15 is cleared
		- Leaving: after clearing Tide 15, the players vote between tides on leaving, and the map is left when a majority votes to
		- Only between tides: the players are only able to leave between tides, so they are not able to exit right before their demise
		- Bonus tides: no limit. They go on, each one harder, until a majority votes to leave. Staying bonus tides allows the player to accumulate more [Pure Aura] -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Pure Aura]
		- Status: built, and passed on the PC: 60 second breaks, a map won once Tide 15 is cleared, the vote to leave, and bonus tides past Tide 29 /~ docs/reports/BUILD_2026-11-03.md \

	[Sea Chart] / Slay the Spire's map \
		- Chart: a campaign's maps sit on a sea chart generated when the campaign starts, like Slay the Spire's map: a harbour, then 10 rows of 3 or 4 base game maps
		- Links: each map links to 1 to 3 maps in the next row, and the 5th and 10th rows close a stretch
		- Maps offered: every base game map can be offered in the vote / Never the custom map slot, which hangs while loading \
		- No repeats: no map is on a chart twice
		- Spread: the chart spreads maps by difficulty: easier maps in the first rows, harder and δ maps later, the hardest closing each stretch
		- Every map is voted on, the first map included
		- Vote: the vote offers the maps linked to the one just played (the first row for map 1). It is held in the map, after the vote to leave; map 1's vote is held in the opening break of a staging map
		- Sailing: the party then returns to the boat, which sails across the chart to the winning map. It loads once everyone readies up, and friends can join on the boat between maps
		- On the boat: between maps, players can't swap their Cardinals or Trinkets
		- Lobby Cardinals: picked once, before the first map, then locked for the campaign; the lobby offers only what the shop may sell -> 12_Cardinals.txt
		- Islands: each map shows its own island from the base game's world maps, at the base game's size, in the home world's water; a map with no island of its own borrows one
		- Chart view: the whole chart shows beside the vote cards, and on a key at any time; ~the cards are lettered A, B, C, and pointing at one lights its island and the routes it leads to~. The boat shows ~a banner naming the map it sails to and a mini chart~ /~ Designed, being built: docs/mode/CHART_NAVIGATION.md \
		- Look: the game's own UI: ~square tiles, teal for a map with its Danger, coral for a stretch's last map, pixel icons, white corner brackets on the island picked~
		- Rest and treasure stops: one {Cove} and one {Wreck} in each stretch. Not every route meets one, so taking one is a route choice
		- Old campaigns: a campaign saved before the stops keeps its chart without them
		- Status: built, and passed on the PC, the in-map vote and the boat's trip included. How the chart looks is yours to check by eye /~ docs/mode/SEA_CHART.md sections 7-9; docs/reports/BUILD_2026-11-03.md \

		{Cove}
			- What: a rest stop. No map: the boat sails there, the party rests, and the boat sails on
			- Where: the row before each stretch's last map, rows ~4 and 9~, on one island the chart picks
			- Gives: ~a retry back~, up to 3. With all 3 retries left, ~each player's lowest-ranked held Direction goes up a rank~ instead
			- Vote: voted like a map; when it wins, the same vote asks at once where to sail after it
			- Counting: the campaign is still 10 rows. Difficulty follows the row, not the maps played (~by row~), so a rest skips a map's pay, not its climb
			- Never lost: a Cove is never lost or replayed, and its gift is never given twice
			- Icon: ~a campfire~
			- Status: designed, being built /~ docs/mode/CHART_NAVIGATION.md section 4.1 \

		{Wreck}
			- What: a treasure stop: a map like the others, calm (no weather)
			- Where: the middle of each stretch, rows ~2-3 and 6-8~, on one island the chart picks; never rows 1, 5 or 10
			- Harder: it plays ~one row harder~: the next row's Tides, foe health and modifiers
			- Pays: when it is won, each player picks ~one Trinket of 3~ the shop may sell and they don't hold, free. A Trinket held raises later foes' health like any other -> [Difficulty]
			- Lost: as any map: a retry is spent and only the Wreck can be picked until it is won
			- Icon: ~a chest~
			- Status: designed, being built /~ docs/mode/CHART_NAVIGATION.md section 4.2 \

	[Sea Weather]
		- Which maps: some maps on the chart carry a weather, rolled with the chart
		- Calm rows: the first row and the rows closing a stretch are calm
		- Odds: about 1 map in 3 has weather in the first stretch, 1 in 2 in the second
		- Pays: 1 extra [Pure Aura] each tide, on top of what the tide pays: 3 for Tides 1-15, 2 for a bonus tide -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Pure Aura]
		- Shown: the weather shows on its island on the chart, and its in-game line in the boat's map panel when the map is picked; the map's start announces only its name (RISING WATER ROLLS IN)
		- Never twice: a map never gets the weather it already has: Manafold already floods, Chrono already speeds up, Vultures already has a nest
		- Status: built, and passed its PC round. How a flooded map looks is yours to check by eye

		{Rising Water}
			- In-game line: Rising Water: the water climbs each Tide. More Pure Aura
			- Does: the water climbs each tide. Flooded tiles can't be built on, and shots through the water slow down.
			- Numbers: the water rises each tide by its own stats, as on Manafold
			- Never on: Manafold
			- Look: a teal swell with a foam crest

		{Quickening Current}
			- In-game line: Quickening Current: the game runs faster from Tide 5. More Pure Aura
			- Does: from Tide 5 the whole game runs faster, and faster again from Tide 10.
			- Numbers: x1.25 from Tide 5, x1.5 from Tide 10
			- Never on: Chrono
			- Look: three foam chevrons

		{Nesting Ground}
			- In-game line: Nesting Ground: a halberd nest grows near the foes. More Pure Aura
			- Does: a halberd nest sits near where the enemies come from. It grows each tide and sends out a halberd every few tides.
			- Numbers: placed 3 to 7 tiles from an enemy core
			- Never on: Vultures
			- Look: a coral egg in a sand nest
