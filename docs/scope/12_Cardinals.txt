	[Rules]
		- What: a new Cardinal or Trinket is a new run modifier, the same kind of effect as the base game's Cardinals
		- Names: found objects the wave washed up, two plain words each
		- Bought: from the shopkeeper with [Pure Aura] -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Shopkeeper]
		- Two kinds: Cardinals and Trinkets, kept apart in the shop and in the list /~ Named on 2026-11-04 at your word: a Trinket was a Vessel Cardinal and a Cardinal a Team Cardinal \
		- Trinket: belongs to the player who bought it, acts only on that player's vessel, towers, units or aura, and shows on their vessel's panel /~ Every base game Cardinal the shop sells stays a Cardinal; only new ones are Trinkets \
		- Cardinal: acts on the core, the enemies or the Tide clock, so it is everyone's and shows in the Cardinal row at the top of the screen. A Cardinal is bought by one player and works for everyone /~ Every base game Cardinal the shop sells is one: it goes on the core \
		- No limit: there is no limit on how many different Cardinals or Trinkets a campaign holds. Past the Cardinal row's 8, the rest show as a +N count beside it
		- Stacking: a new Cardinal or Trinket that stacks works for up to 6 copies and leaves the shop at 6; a base game Cardinal is bought once and then leaves the shop
		- Saved: Trinkets and Directions are saved with the campaign for each player and come back when it continues
		- On the boat: no Cardinal or Trinket swaps between maps -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Sea Chart]
		- Power: a Trinket is worth about one level 1 or 2 building upgrade, spread over one player's things; a Cardinal about one base game Cardinal such as Vintage Watch, Wave Breaker or Aura Gavel, or less /~ docs/reports/DIRECTIONS_CARDINALS_2026-11-04.md section 2 \
		- Pairs: two Cardinals or Trinkets that clash are a pair, like a research choice: a player who holds one is never offered the other
		- No copies: a new Cardinal or Trinket never does what a base game Cardinal already does, even on other things /~ Checked against the 40 base game Cardinals' own in-game lines on 2026-11-04; your word that day removed the twelve of Claude's that did \
		- By: each Cardinal and Trinket says who made it, Dimitri or Claude -> 00_Read_Me.txt [Values]
		- Kept names: Model Lighthouse, your name for the old start Cardinal, is kept for a later Cardinal or item
