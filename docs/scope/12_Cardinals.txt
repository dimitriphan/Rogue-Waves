	[Rules]
		- What: a new Cardinal is a new run modifier, the same kind of effect as the base game's Cardinals
		- Names: found objects the wave washed up, two plain words each / Cracked Buoy, Ship's Bell \
		- Bought: from the shopkeeper with [Pure Aura] -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Shopkeeper]
		- Two kinds: kept apart in the shop and in the Cardinal List
		- Vessel Cardinal: belongs to the player who bought it, acts only on that player's vessel, towers, units or aura, and shows on their vessel's panel /~ Vessel wherever the game allows it. About 24 of the 40 stock Cardinals can be made Vessel Cardinals; see docs/SCOPE_VS_LOOP.md §F (read from the game's code, not yet seen in game) \
		- Team Cardinal: acts on the core, the enemies or the Tide clock, so it is everyone's and shows in the Cardinal row at the top of the screen. A Team Cardinal is bought by one player and works for everyone
		- No limit: Cardinals scale forever: there is no limit on how many Team or Vessel Cardinals a campaign holds /~ The game's Cardinal row keeps a counter for only 8, lobby picks included. Past 8, Team Cardinals still work, and the mode shows them another way; that is settled when the shop is built \
		- Stacking: Cardinals stack with no limit: a Cardinal that stacks can be bought again as many times as a player can pay for it
		- Saved: Vessel Cardinals and Directions are saved with the campaign for each player and come back when it continues
		- On the boat: no Cardinal swaps between maps -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Sea Chart]
		- Kept names: Model Lighthouse, your name for the old start Cardinal, is kept for a later Cardinal or item
