| New Vessel: Yossele |
	Yossele, The Patient Golem
		# Keeping your towers alive grants bonuses to them, with a "growing defense" gameplay style. Scaling archetype
		# Yossele is slightly more powerful statwise, which allows them to defend their base as their towers ramp up in effectiveness. / More health, damage, and health regen \
		# Can gain fertilizer that allows Yossele to speed up growth of a tower. Fertilizer is only unlocked if a [Direction] is picked that includes it.
		# Gameplay loops plays around time management and slow growth, and keeping your important buildings safe.
		# Look: round clay head with a see-through hole for a mouth, a two-leaf sprout, glowing green eyes, four long root-like limbs. Towers carry round holes and root feet, and each [Growth] stage adds leaves or sprouts.
		# Status: concept page done. Next up after Argo. / Difficulty default: Intermediate \
		# Full sprite sheet (idle, move, back view, fall, land, attack) comes once the design is past drafting. / One front facing concept frame so far, in sprites \
		# Attack: melee.
		# Ability: {Fertilize}, which spends fertilizer to speed up a tower's [Growth]. It is unlocked by a [Direction], and its button doesn't show until Yossele has it.
		# [Growth] rises with each tide a tower survives, and resets if the tower is destroyed.
		# Fertilizer is earned through the requirements of Yossele's own [Directions]. / Compost Bin, in 11_Directions.txt, is the first \
		# The hole in Yossele's head is their mouth.
		/ Lore comes once every character is fully realized \

		(Offense 1):{Mushroom Cannon}
			# Slowly stacks spores on enemies, dealing DOT. Long range cannon
			- Lobs long range spore bombs that deal AOE damage and stack spores / Uses arcs, similar to Smacky's bomb towers. \
			- DOT increases as spores stack. / Default maximum stacks of spores is 10 \
			- As {Mushroom Cannon} experiences [Growth], its attack speed and explosion AOE increases. / Max [Growth] is 5, attack speed per growth and AOE radius per growth are to be determined. \
			- Full [Growth] causes spores to bounce once after landing, exploding again in the same spot 1 second later at half effectiveness. / Half AOE range and half damage for the bouncing spore bomb. \
				% 1:{Sticky Spores} Reaching max stacks of spores slows enemies by a percentage. / 25% speed reduction \
				% 1:{Farout Fungus} Range increases, with spore bombs increases in explosion AOE depending on how long the projectile is in the air.
				
				% 2:{Viral Outbreak} Enemies that die with max stacks of spores have a chance to explode with a spore bomb. / 30% chance \
				% 2:{Deathcap Training} Increases damage over time effect from spores, and reduces maximum amount of stacks. / DOT increased by 25%, while maximum spore stacks is reduced to 8 \
				
				% 3:{Explosive Finish} When reaching maximum spore stacks, enemies explode with spores, consuming spore stacks and dealing AOE damage based on how many spore stacks were reached. This explosion also can apply spore stacks.
				% 3:{Hallucinogen} Enemies that have maximum spore stacks have a chance to attack their allies for a small period of time. / While at max spore stacks, every 5 seconds has a 20% chance to attack their allies for 2 seconds. \
