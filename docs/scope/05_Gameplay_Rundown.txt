| Gameplay Rundown |
	# A campaign step by step, from the main menu to the last map. Each step points to the file holding its rules.

	[Steps]
		- 1. Lobby: Players are first grouped together in a host's campaign, where players must select their vessel first before the Rogue Waves begins as they are locked into their choice. The host starts a campaign from the Rogue Waves button on the main menu (New or Continue), which takes them to the boat with the mode already set, and invites friends there. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Campaign]
		- 2. Direction: After all players are ready, the Rogue Waves begins and each player is given a choice of 3 new upgrades called [Direction] and picks 1. These upgrades range from tower stat increases, to major gameplay alterations that fundamentally change how a Vessel plays. -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Directions]
		- 3. Vote: Once each player selects their [Direction], players vote on the next map, and their boat sails across a sea chart to it. Every map is voted on, the first map included. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Sea Chart]
		- 4. In the map: Once in game, gameplay remains similar with new objectives that tie into the new currency [Pure Aura] and shop mechanic. [Pure Aura] is earned each tide, with optional objectives that players can take on for bonus [Pure Aura], similar to the bounty mechanic from the base game. -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Pure Aura], 14_Optional_Objectives.txt
		- 5. Breaks: By completing these objectives, players earn extra [Pure Aura] which can be traded to the shopkeeper {VENDCINT} in between tides for [Cardinals], [Trinkets] or ranks of [Directions] they already hold. -> 06_New_Mechanics.txt [Shopkeeper], 12_Cardinals.txt
		- 6. Leaving: After clearing Tide 15, the players vote between tides on leaving, and the map is left when a majority votes to. However, staying bonus tides allows the player to accumulate more [Pure Aura], and bonus tides go on, each one harder, until a majority votes to leave. The players are only able to leave between tides, so they are not able to exit right before their demise. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Tides and Leaving]
		- 7. Next map: The loop then continues map after map of choosing a [Direction], playing the map, and escaping for a total of 10 maps with increasing difficulty. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Difficulty]
		- 8. Between maps: Between maps the campaign is saved: the players can stop there and continue later, and when the host picks the campaign up again with Continue on the main menu's Rogue Waves button each player gets back their vessel, [Pure Aura], [Directions] and [Trinkets], and the team its [Cardinals]. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Campaign]
		- 9. The end: If the players clear Tide 15 on the final map and leave it, they complete the campaign and a new Depth level is unlocked. A failed campaign keeps nothing. -> 04_Rogue_Waves_Mode.txt [Campaign], [Depth]
