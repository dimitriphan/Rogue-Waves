		[Identity]
			- Title: Deep Sea Diver
			- Difficulty: Intermediate
			- Archetype: No walls; control the field instead. Slow foes, pin them, reel them in, and blast what's stuck
			- In-game line: Sturdy, slow melee, hits two at once
			- Look: bulky dome-helmeted diver with hands touching the body
			- Towers look: low-poly and sleek: brass domes, portholes and rivets on the top and front faces
			- Accent colour: sea blue
			- Sprites: as shipped in the earlier ArgoMod
			- Lore: Argo went down to find the bottom of the Basin and never quite came back up. Heavy boots, a heavier wrench, and a crew that fixes anything that stands still long enough.
			- Status: Argo 2.0.1 designed, built, passes its PC self-test, 144 of 144 in the latest round, and played on a second player's machine. The 2026-11-04 review's changes below are not built yet. Ships only inside the Rogue Waves pack.
