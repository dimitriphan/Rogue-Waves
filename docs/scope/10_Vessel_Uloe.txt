		[Vessel Mechanic] [Light]
			- Rule: Towers have increased effects based on how much light they're receiving. {Photon}s and Uloe provide light around them.
			- {Illuminate}: {Photon}s can {Illuminate} enemies, increasing their damage taken.
			- {Blind}: Can also {Blind} enemies, reducing their damage.
			- Strength: {Illuminate} and {Blind} have different levels of strength depending on its source.
			- Details: /~ The game has no light for gameplay, so the mod adds up the light from sources in range (docs/RESEARCH_GAPS.md B1) \
			- Sources: ~Uloe Light 1 within 3 tiles; a {Photon} Light 1 within 2 tiles; {Plug In} and the {Flood Light} as written on them~
			- Cap: ~12~ Light counted per building
			- Effects: ~{Magnifying Glass} +20% damage per Light; {Power Bank}'s {Photon}s come back 10% faster per Light; {Mirror} at level 1 from Light 1, level 2 from Light 3, level 3 from Light 6; {Flood Light} mines 10% faster per Light~
			- Levels: ~{Blind} -15% / -30% / -45% damage dealt, {Illuminate} +15% / +30% / +45% damage taken, lasting 3 / 4 / 5 seconds; a foe holds one level of each, and a stronger one replaces a weaker~
