| New Vessel: Uloe |
	Uloe, Light Personified
		# Uses {Light} as a mechanic
		# Uses wandering minions named {Photons}, similar to Sno Sno, that interact with towers.
		# {Photons} can {Illuminate} enemies, increasing their damage taken. Can also {Blind} enemies, reducing their damage. {Illuminate} and {Blind} have different levels of strength depending on its source.
		# Towers have increased effects based on how much light they're receiving. {Photons} and Uloe provide light around them.
		# Look: glass light bulb head with a face for a filament, a ribbed screw base on a tall lamp post, yellow floating arms made of electricity at the sides, a cord to a plug. Towers carry glass, screw rings and cords.
		# Status: concept page done. Comes last. The game has no light for gameplay, so the mod adds up the light from sources in range (docs/RESEARCH_GAPS.md B1). / Difficulty default: Advanced \
		# Full sprite sheet (idle, move, back view, fall, land, attack) comes once the design is past drafting. / One front facing concept frame so far, in sprites \
		# Attack: ranged.
		# Ability: {Plug In}. Uloe plugs into a node or a {Power Bank} and gives off high levels of {Light} in a radius around them, as long as they stay in range of what they plugged into. / The level of {Light} depends on the node's total aura or the {Power Bank}'s upgrades \
		# The plug and cord are the {Plug In} ability's: they move with Uloe, like a cord in real life.
		# The yellow strokes are floating arms made of electricity.
		/ Lore comes once the character is finalized \

		(Minions):{Photons}
			# Refers to minion units
		(Offense):{Magnifying Glass}
			- Focuses beams of light to deal damage to enemies.
			- Damage dependent on how much {Light} it is receiving
				% Damage increases as it attacks the same target
		(Support):{Power Bank}
			- Spawns {Photons} which are light minions that provide benefits to towers.
			- {Photons} wander around aimlessly where they spawn, but will follow light paths from {Flood Light}s if they're being directed.
			- Can attack enemies for very little damage, but apply illumination and blinding.
				% Photons explode on death, dealing damage and applying blind + illumination
		(Defense):{Mirror}
			- Wall that can be empowered by {Light} to blind enemies, reducing their damage.
				% {Blind} or {Illuminate} enemies based on upgrade path.
				% Gain a chance to completely nullify damage based on levels of {Blind} on opponent.
				% A portion of {Light} directed to {Mirror} is added to nearby towers that can be empowered by {Light}
		(Economy):{Flood Light}
			- Plugs into nodes and provides light. Can be directed towards towers to focus their light benefits.
				% Grants light in a radius around itself. Can no longer be directed.
