| Rogue Waves Mode |
	The Rogue Waves is an expansive mod revolving around roguelike mechanics and new content. New vessels and cardinals are included, along with a new roguelike mode. In this mode, players join together and play a campaign of 10 maps in a row with increasing difficulty and new modifiers / enemies that change with each campaign. Progress is saved between maps, so a campaign can be played over several sessions. Players can empower themselves using new mechanics and upgrades, purchasable with a new currency earned each tide and from optional objectives given to the player: [Directions] are picked before each map, and their ranks are bought at {VENDCINT}. Completing a campaign unlocks a harder difficulty modifier for future campaigns. Score does not count in this mode.
