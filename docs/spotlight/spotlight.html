The Rogue Waves mode

One campaign, start to finish

The host starts a run from the main menu. Every map after that follows the same loop: pick a Direction, vote, sail, hold the tides, spend Pure Aura in the breaks, and leave once Tide 15 is behind you.

  1. Board the boat

    The Rogue Waves button on the main menu offers New or Continue and takes the host to the boat with the mode set. Friends join there. Each player picks a vessel and is locked into it for the run.

  2. Pick a Direction

    Before every map each player is offered 3 Directions and keeps 1. Some raise a stat; Shifts change how a vessel plays.

  3. Vote and sail

    The whole sea chart opens beside the vote. The islands in reach are lettered A, B, C; hover one to light every lane it leads to. The boat then sails there, a banner naming where it goes.

  4. Hold the tides

    The map plays like the base game, harder. Every tide pays each player Pure Aura, and optional objectives pay more.

  5. Shop in the break

    Breaks last 60 seconds. VENDCINT drops in to sell Cardinals, Trinkets and Direction ranks, and to carry aura between friends.

  6. Leave or stay

    Clearing Tide 15 wins the map. From then on the party votes between tides: leave, or stay for bonus tides that pay more and hit harder. Nobody can leave mid-tide.

  7. Save and go on

    The host's save keeps the run between maps. Continue brings back each vessel, Pure Aura, Directions and Trinkets, and the team's Cardinals. Three retries replay a lost map, and a Cove on the chart gives one back.

Difficulty

Ten maps, each harder than the last

The run must end harder than the base game, even with every Cardinal and Direction the party stacks up. Each map's Tide 15 plays a later base-game tide, foe health climbs, and the seed switches on its foe modifiers one at a time.

Foe modifiers on

On top of that: foes +6% a map and +50% for each player past the first; spawns 3% quicker a map; health +2% for every Cardinal, Trinket copy and Direction rank the party holds when a map starts.

Modifiers per campaign: the seed draws 2 of the 4 new modifiers and 2 of the stock elites (Armor, Health, Red, Explode, Aegis). They ride on one foe in five, taken in turn from tide to tide.

Bonus tides play the rows after Tide 15 with the map's scaling, and from row 29 each one adds 10% more foes. They never cost a retry: only the tide in progress pays nothing.

Between maps

A new sea chart every run

A roguelike map screen, sailed by the game's own boat. A harbour, then 10 rows of 3 or 4 base-game maps, each linked to 1 to 3 in the next row. Every vote opens the whole chart, so the party sees where each island leads before it picks.

  • SpreadEasier maps in the first rows, harder and δ maps later, the hardest closing each stretch of five.
  • No repeatsNo map sits on a chart twice. Every base-game map can come up.
  • The voteHeld in the map after the vote to leave, with the chart on the left and the lettered cards on the right. Click a lit island or pick a card.
  • Any timePress M on the boat or in a break to open the chart and read the route ahead.
  • SailingA banner says where the boat goes, with its Danger and weather, and a mini chart inks the lane as it sails.
  • After a lossThe boat sails back by itself, and only the lost map can be picked until it is won.
  • On the boatNo Cardinal or Trinket swaps. Lobby Cardinals are picked once, before map 1, and stay for the run.
C

The Cove

A rest stop, one in each stretch, on the row before its Breaker. No map: the party rests and gets a retry back. With all three retries left, each player's lowest held Direction goes up a rank instead.

Resting skips a map's pay, not its climb: the next map is as hard as its row.

W

The Wreck

A treasure stop in the middle of each stretch. A calm map that plays one row harder. Win it and each player picks one Trinket of three, free.

Every Trinket held makes later foes tougher, so the prize is also a cost.

Sea weather is rolled with the chart: about 1 map in 3 in the first stretch, 1 in 2 in the second, never on the first row, a row that closes a stretch, a Cove or a Wreck. A weather map pays 1 extra Pure Aura each tide.

A look per weather: each weather brings its own light and fog, fading in over 4 seconds after the map's intro, on every player's screen. Calm maps keep their own light.

Between tides

Breaks, the shop and Pure Aura

VENDCINT

A sentient vending machine that falls from the sky by the vessels' landing spot at the start of every break and jumps back out when it ends. It sells Cardinals, Trinkets and the next rank of a Direction you hold, hands out the optional objective, and runs Fund Transfer. Each buy costs 3 Pure Aura more for every Cardinal the team holds and every Trinket copy the buyer holds.

Pure Aura

A new currency for the run, one counter per player in a pearl white none of the base auras use.

3each tide, 1 to 15
2each bonus tide
+1a tide on a weather map

Direction rank II costs 20, rank III 30; a base-game Cardinal 15.

Optional objectives

A new bounty is offered at VENDCINT in every break from the one before Tide 3. It lasts the next tide, pays every player in full, and failing it costs nothing.

Fund Transfer

Friends can send each other base aura at VENDCINT in a break, up to 50 times the coming tide's number, in any mix of colours: 50 before Tide 1, 250 before Tide 5. Pure Aura never moves. This is the expansion's economy change, and it lets a team split into roles.

Retries

Three a campaign. A lost map is replayed from the last save; with none left, the run ends and keeps nothing. Only the host quitting mid-map spends one. A Cove gives one back. A friend who drops can rejoin the running map.

Online

Built for a crew of two to four

Playing together

  • The host runs the run; everyone needs the same pack version.
  • Rejoin a running map. The host keeps a dropped player's seat (vessel, towers, aura) and reopens the lobby. A game restart keeps the seat too.
  • Load watchdog. One player who can't load a map is sent back, instead of everyone going to the main menu.

Clearer screens

  • A campaign card on the loading screen, an intro card for each map, a world card on the chart and an end screen title.
  • Retry warnings: the host's exit dialog names the cost, and the end screen counts the retries left.
  • A "has no mods on" warning on the boat, and a rejoin line in a dropped player's error box.
  • Weather icons over chart islands, and the pack's tips in the game's tip lists.

Every screen alike

  • One sea, one light. Each weather's fog, ambient light and sun fade in on every player's screen at once.
  • Every foe on every screen, even for a friend whose first map is a join.
  • The campaign's title crowns the win screen on every machine.
  • F picks your vessel, even after it drowns.
