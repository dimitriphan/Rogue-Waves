The Rogue Waves mode

One campaign, start to finish

The host starts a run from the main menu. Every map after that follows the same loop: pick a Direction, vote, sail, hold the tides, spend Pure Aura in the breaks, and leave once Tide 15 is behind you. Played on the PC

  1. Board the boat

    The Rogue Waves button on the main menu offers New or Continue and takes the host to the boat with the mode set. Friends join there. Each player picks a vessel and is locked into it for the run.

  2. Pick a Direction

    Before every map each player is offered 3 Directions and keeps 1. Some raise a stat; Shifts change how a vessel plays.

  3. Vote and sail

    The party votes on the next island among the ones linked on the sea chart. The boat then sails there by itself.

  4. Hold the tides

    The map plays like the base game, harder. Every tide pays each player Pure Aura, and optional objectives pay more.

  5. Shop in the break

    Breaks last 60 seconds. VENDCINT drops in to sell Cardinals, Trinkets and Direction ranks, and to carry aura between friends.

  6. Leave or stay

    Clearing Tide 15 wins the map. From then on the party votes between tides: leave, or stay for bonus tides that pay more and hit harder. Nobody can leave mid-tide.

  7. Save and go on

    The host's save keeps the run between maps. Continue brings back each vessel, Pure Aura, Directions and Trinkets, and the team's Cardinals. Three retries replay a lost map.

Foe modifiers on

On top of that: foes +6% a map and +50% for each player past the first; spawns 3% quicker a map; health +2% for every Cardinal, Trinket copy and Direction rank the party holds when a map starts.

Modifiers per campaign: the seed draws 2 of the 4 new modifiers and 2 of the stock elites (Armor, Health, Red, Explode, Aegis). They ride on one foe in five, taken in turn from tide to tide.

Bonus tides play the rows after Tide 15 with the map's scaling, and from row 29 each one adds 10% more foes. They never cost a retry: only the tide in progress pays nothing.

Between maps

A new sea chart every run

Like a roguelike map screen, sailed by the game's own boat. A harbour, then 10 rows of 3 or 4 base-game maps, each linked to 1 to 3 in the next row. Played on the PC

  • SpreadEasier maps in the first rows, harder and δ maps later, the hardest closing each stretch of five.
  • No repeatsNo map sits on a chart twice. Every base-game map can come up; the custom slot never does.
  • The voteHeld in the map, after the vote to leave. Map 1's vote is held in the opening break of a staging map.
  • After a lossThe boat sails back by itself, and only the lost map can be picked until it is won.
  • On the boatNo Cardinal or Trinket swaps. Lobby Cardinals are picked once, before map 1, and stay for the run.
  • The chart's lookIslands at the base game's size in the home world's water, a flag and dashed lanes in the pack's colours. Still his to check by eye

Between tides

Breaks, the shop and Pure Aura

VENDCINT

Played on the PC

A sentient vending machine that falls from the sky by the vessels' landing spot at the start of every break and jumps back out when it ends. It sells Cardinals, Trinkets and the next rank of a Direction you hold, hands out the optional objective, and runs Fund Transfer. Each buy costs 3 Pure Aura more for every Cardinal the team holds and every Trinket copy the buyer holds. Its look is temporary art.

Pure Aura

Played on the PC

A new currency for the run, one counter per player in a pearl white none of the base auras use.

3each tide, 1 to 15
2each bonus tide
+1a tide on a weather map

Direction rank II costs 20, rank III 30; a base-game Cardinal 15.

Fund Transfer

Played with a second player

Friends can send each other base aura at VENDCINT in a break, up to 50 times the coming tide's number, in any mix of colours: 50 before Tide 1, 250 before Tide 5. Pure Aura never moves. This is the expansion's economy change, and it lets a team split into roles.

Retries

Played on the PC

Three a campaign. A lost map is replayed from the last save; with none left, the run ends and keeps nothing. Only the host quitting mid-map spends one. A friend who drops can rejoin the running map.

Four new vessels

Yossele, Spool, Argo and Uloe

Each one has its own towers, upgrade trees, minions and Directions, and plays outside the mode too. Spool's and Uloe's art is temporary; Yossele's sheets are final drafts waiting for the PC check; Argo keeps his shipped look.

Online and under the hood

Built for a crew of two to four

Playing together

Played with a second player
  • The host runs the run; everyone needs the same pack version.
  • Rejoin a running map. The host keeps a dropped player's seat (vessel, towers, aura) and reopens the lobby. A game restart keeps the seat too.
  • Load watchdog. One player who can't load a map is sent back, instead of everyone going to the main menu.
  • Steam's own Join Game and a real network's drops wait for an online evening. Not tried yet

Clearer screens

Played on the PC
  • A campaign card on the loading screen, an intro card for each map, a world card on the chart and an end screen title.
  • Retry warnings: the host's exit dialog names the cost, and the end screen counts the retries left.
  • A "has no mods on" warning on the boat, and a rejoin line in a dropped player's error box.
  • Weather icons over chart islands, and the pack's tips in the game's tip lists.

Fixes and lighting

  • F picks the vessel again. After a death, F no longer selects a minion such as Boutbro. Fix built, the next PC round checks it
  • No way back in after a drop is fixed by the rejoin above. Played on the PC
  • Map lighting. The kit can now change a map's fog, ambient light and sun at run time, which the weather looks use. 23 of 23 on the PC

Shipping

How it reaches players

First release: Yossele alone

The RogueWavesMod pack holding only Yossele, version 0.1.0, as a zip you share yourself (the game has no Workshop). Yossele plays in a normal game; Fertilize waits for the mode, since only a Direction unlocks it. Ships after Dimitri's play test, his look at the sprites, his balance pass and his word.

After it: everything else

The Rogue Waves mode with its sea chart and weather, Argo, Spool, Uloe, the modifiers, Directions, Cardinals and Trinkets, each added to the same pack so players update one mod.
