| Rogue Wave Mode |
	The Rogue Wave is an expansive mod revolving around roguelike mechanics and new content. New vessels and cardinals are included, along with a new roguelike mode. In this mode, players join together and complete 3 maps in a row with increasing difficulty and new modifiers / enemies that change with each run. Players can empower themselves using new mechanics and upgrades, purchasable with a new currency found in game from doing objectives given to the player. Completing a run unlocks a harder difficulty modifier for future runs.
	
| Gameplay Rundown |
	Players are first grouped together in a host's campaign, where players must select their vessel first before the Rogue Wave begins as they are locked into their choice. After all players are ready, the host then selects the option to begin the Rogue Wave, where each player is given a choice of new upgrades called [Direction]. These upgrades range from tower stat increases, to major gameplay alternations that fundamentally change how a Vessel plays. Once each player selects their [Direction], players are then given a choice of 3 maps from the base game which are then voted on. Once in game, gameplay remains similar with new objectives that tie into the new currency [Pure Aura] and shop mechanic. [Pure Aura] is earned each tide, with optional objectives that players can take on for bonus [Pure Aura], similar to the bounty mechanic from the base game. By completing these objectives, players earn extra [Pure Aura] which can be traded with to a shopkeeper inbetween waves for [Cardinals] or [Directions]. After successfully reaching Tide 15, the map gives the players the option to leave. However, staying bonus tides allows the player to accumulate more [Pure Aura]. The players are only able to leave between tides, so they are not able to exit right before their demise. The loop then continues 2 more times of choosing a direction, playing the map, and escaping for a total of 3 maps with increasing difficulty. If the players are able to stay until the final tide on the final map, they succeed in their run and a new ascension level is unlocked.


| New Mechanics |
	[Directions]
		A new gameplay altering mechanic specific to each player. Directions can provide normal bonuses to characters, or create fundamental changes to how a vessel functions.

	[Pure Aura]
		A new currency earned in game to purchase [Directions] and [Cardinals]. Can be earned by surviving tides and by accepting and completing optional objectives. Each player has their own amount of [Pure Aura].
	
	[Fund Transfer]
		Players are able to send each other a limited amount of aura inbetween waves with the shopkeeper, allowing for more cooperative play and specialization into roles.
