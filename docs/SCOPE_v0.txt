| Rogue Wave Mode |
	The Rogue Wave is an expansive mod revolving around roguelike mechanics and new content. New vessels and cardinals are included, along with a new roguelike mode. In this mode, players join together and play a campaign of 15 maps in a row with increasing difficulty and new modifiers / enemies that change with each campaign. Progress is saved between maps, so a campaign can be played over several sessions. Players can empower themselves using new mechanics and upgrades, purchasable with a new currency found in game from doing objectives given to the player. Completing a campaign unlocks a harder difficulty modifier for future campaigns. Score does not count in this mode.

	/ Swell is on hold until the core gameplay loop is settled (2026-10-09) \

| Gameplay Rundown |
	Players are first grouped together in a host's campaign, where players must select their vessel first before the Rogue Wave begins as they are locked into their choice. The host starts a campaign from the Rogue Wave button on the main menu (New or Continue), which takes them to the boat with the mode already set, and invites friends there. After all players are ready, the Rogue Wave begins and each player is given a choice of 3 new upgrades called [Direction] and picks 1. These upgrades range from tower stat increases, to major gameplay alternations that fundamentally change how a Vessel plays. Once each player selects their [Direction], players are then given a choice of 3 maps from the base game which are then voted on. Every map is voted on, the first map included. Once in game, gameplay remains similar with new objectives that tie into the new currency [Pure Aura] and shop mechanic. [Pure Aura] is earned each tide, with optional objectives that players can take on for bonus [Pure Aura], similar to the bounty mechanic from the base game. By completing these objectives, players earn extra [Pure Aura] which can be traded with to a shopkeeper inbetween waves for [Cardinals] or [Directions]. After successfully reaching Tide 15, the map gives the players the option to leave. However, staying bonus tides allows the player to accumulate more [Pure Aura]. The players are only able to leave between tides, so they are not able to exit right before their demise. The loop then continues map after map of choosing a direction, playing the map, and escaping for a total of 15 maps with increasing difficulty. Between maps the campaign is saved: the players can stop there and continue later, and when the host picks the campaign up again with Continue on the main menu's Rogue Wave button each player gets back their vessel, [Pure Aura], [Directions] and [Cardinals]. If the players are able to stay until the final tide on the final map, they complete the campaign and a new ascension level is unlocked.
