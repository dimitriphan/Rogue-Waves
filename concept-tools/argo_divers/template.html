Argo, a diver who might not be human

Five takes on Argo built on your idea, each drawn over his shipped sprite so the brass dome stays his. Each one changes what is inside the suit, or what the sea has done to it. They are 32 px game-size sprites with a six-frame idle and a back view. Pick one, or write what to mix, and the page keeps your pick for the next build.

Game size, shown 3×:shippedBarnacledAnglerHollowPassengerPressure Hull

Barnacled

the sea grew on him

The same diver, down so long the sea has started keeping him. Verdigris creeps over the brass, barnacles crust the dome's edge and his shoulder, kelp is snagged on the valve and a starfish holds onto his chest.

Still human: mostly
Idle
The kelp sways and a bubble slips out by his cheek.
Towers
Barnacles and green patches on the brass domes.
Watch
The closest to the shipped look, so every 1.4.0 animation can take the same overlay. At 1× the barnacles read as speckle; the kelp and starfish carry it.

Angler

the deep gave him a lure

Part fish. The valve has grown into an anglerfish lure that arches over the porthole. Behind the glass are wide-set fish eyes and a long mouth with two small teeth. A spiny fin runs down the back of the dome, and webbed fins hang where his mitts were.

Still human: half
Idle
The lure glows and dips, and the near fin flicks.
Kit fit
A lure suits the Harpoon's reel and pull. Reel and Undertow could flash it.
Watch
The lure takes him to 28 px, the tallest a vessel can be. The wrench swing needs a fin that grips.
23 × 28 px · 16 colours

Hollow

nobody in the suit, only sea

The suit came back up, but nobody is inside. The helmet is full of seawater, with a pocket of air at the top, a bright surface line, two pale lights drifting at different heights and a small fish swimming through. The faded suit has lost its square shoulders and the empty gloves hang limp at its sides.

Still human: none
Idle
The fish crosses the glass, the lights drift, the last air bubbles out of the valve and a glove drips.
Lore
Fits his line as written: he "never quite came back up".
Watch
The quietest take. From a distance it can pass for the shipped Argo, and the water in the porthole has to carry it.
19 × 27 px · 14 colours

Passenger

something else wears the suit

Something else is driving. One big gold eye with an octopus's slit pupil fills the porthole. Coral tentacles come out of the cuffs and curl back to the hips, where his mitts used to rest, and a third one feels about from under the collar.

Still human: none, but someone is home
Idle
The tentacle tips curl, the pupil slides toward you and the eye blinks.
Kit fit
Grabbing arms suit pins, slows and pulls.
Watch
The coral sits near Storm Coral, the pack's danger mark. On a vessel it reads as a creature, but the hue can move toward peach if it competes. The tentacles widen him to 24 px.
24 × 27 px · 16 colours

Pressure Hull

a later suit, and what glows in it

A different era of suit: a rounder hard suit made for crushing depths, with a steel barrel body, ball joints and pincer hands resting on the hips. On top is a smaller brass dome with one big port and a little side port. There is no face, only a light that pulses where one should be, and the seam down the chest breathes with it.

Still human: hard to say
Idle
The light swells to white and fades, and the chest seam glows in time with it.
Towers
Steel, rivets and the same cyan glow in the portholes.
Watch
The biggest change from 1.4.0, because the blue suit and the mitts go. The white steel sits near Sno Sno's white, so the dome has to carry who he is. The cyan is Buster's collar teal from the shared palette.
20 × 27 px · 11 colours
